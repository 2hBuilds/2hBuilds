# 2hBuilds

RuneLite plugins for Old School RuneScape. To install one, open RuneLite, click the Plugin Hub (plug icon), search the name and click Install.

## 2h Bank Portfolio Tracker

See what your bank is worth and how it moves.

- Price changes for every item over 1, 7, 30, 90 or 180 days, in gp and %
- Net Worth History: your bank's total saved once a day, as a chart
- Counts your inventory, worn gear and Grand Exchange offers
- Live prices for items that trade a lot

[Plugin Hub page](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/bank-portfolio-tracker) · [Source and issues](https://github.com/2hBuilds/bank-portfolio-tracker)

## 2h CA Boss Checklist

Every Combat Achievements boss and its tasks, in one checklist.

- Finished bosses, and any you mark, show green in the game's Tasks menu
- Each boss's tasks with their tier and type

[Plugin Hub page](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/ca-boss-checklist) · [Source and issues](https://github.com/2hBuilds/ca-boss-checklist)

## 2h Chat Hover Info

Hover a broadcast in your chat for a card about it.

- Drops: the item's price, alch value and drop rates
- Quests, combat tasks, pets, diaries, levels, kill counts and personal bests
- What a new level unlocks, and the wiki's first paragraph on every card
- Right-click a line for "Open wiki" and "Copy"

[Plugin Hub page](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/chat-hover-info) · [Source and issues](https://github.com/2hBuilds/chat-hover-info)

## 2h Chat Mentions

Catch up after being afk.

- Chat lines that name you or a keyword become cards in the sidebar
- Click a card to jump to its line in the chat

[Plugin Hub page](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/chat-mentions) · [Source and issues](https://github.com/2hBuilds/chat-mentions)

## 2h Inventory Setups Quick Check

One click at the bank: what's missing from your Inventory Setups setup.

- A box of each missing item with its count
- Bank items numbered with how many to take out
- Potions that aren't full outlined, even with no setup open
- 13 button looks and a live outline colour

[Plugin Hub page](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/inventory-quick-check) · [Source and issues](https://github.com/2hBuilds/inventory-quick-check)

## 2h Why Lag

Says what caused the lag and what to do about it.

- Your PC, your connection or the world
- Read from frames, server ticks and ping together

[Plugin Hub page](https://runelite.net/plugin-hub/show/why-lag) · [Source and issues](https://github.com/2hBuilds/why-lag)
